Operating principles

Three rules we don't break.

01

Evidence over claims.

If we didn't measure it, we don't print it. Every number on this site traces to something you can check.

02

Scope in writing.

Fixed scope and price before work starts. A change gets priced before it gets built.

03

Plain language.

If we can't explain a decision without jargon, we don't understand it well enough yet.
