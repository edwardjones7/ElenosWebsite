Voice agents

A phone that answers itself.

Voice agents are the fastest win for most service businesses. An AI receptionist picks up every call — after hours, mid-job, during the rush — answers the common questions in a natural voice, books the appointment straight onto your calendar, and texts you a summary. Missed calls are missed revenue; a voice agent makes sure the phone is never the reason you lost the job.

We build them scoped to your business: your services, your pricing rules, your escalation triggers. Anything the agent shouldn't handle gets handed to a human immediately, with the full context attached.

Why it works

Narrow scope, real guardrails.

The AI projects that fail are the vague ones — "add AI to the business" with no specific task, no owner, and no way to tell if it worked. We do the opposite: pick the one task that eats the most hours, automate exactly that, measure the time it returns, and then move to the next one. Speed compounds — a lead answered in ninety seconds converts at a different rate than one answered tomorrow.

Responsible deployment is part of the build, not an afterthought: constrained scopes, human escalation paths, and logs you can audit. Not sure which task to automate first? That's what our AI consulting is for — a short engagement that maps your workflows and ranks the automation opportunities by return, often as part of a systems audit. And when the operator needs a home, our custom software work gives it one.

Questions

Asked before the call.

What can AI actually automate in a small business?

The recurring manual tasks: answering and routing phone calls, following up with leads within minutes instead of days, sorting and responding to email, pulling data out of documents, drafting content, and keeping the CRM current. If a task happens the same way every week, it's a candidate.

Will AI replace my staff?

No — it replaces the task, not the human. The goal is that your team stops doing the repetitive work software should do and spends the time on work that actually needs a person: selling, serving, deciding.

How do you keep AI from saying something wrong to a customer?

Guardrails are part of every build: constrained scopes, human handoff for anything sensitive, logging you can review, and escalation paths. We deploy narrow operators with clear jobs — not an open-ended chatbot pointed at your customers.

What does an AI integration cost?

Scoped and priced fixed after a free discovery call, sized to the task it automates. A single focused operator — say, lead follow-up — is a small build; a multi-workflow system scales from there. You'll have a number within 48 hours.

Do we need custom software first?

Not necessarily. Many operators plug into the tools you already run — your phone line, inbox, calendar, or CRM. When a build does need a foundation, we build that too, and the audit tells us which order to do it in.

What is an AI voice agent?

Software that answers your business phone in a natural voice — 24/7. It handles the common questions, qualifies the caller, books appointments onto your calendar, and hands anything sensitive to a human with full context. For most service businesses it pays for itself in recovered missed calls.
