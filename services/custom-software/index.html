Why it works

Built around your process, not a template.

Off-the-shelf software makes you adapt to it. That's fine until the workarounds pile up — the exported CSVs, the duplicate data entry, the one spreadsheet only Dave understands. Custom software flips it: the tool matches the process, the data lives in one place, and the handoffs that used to leak time and money just happen.

We start small on purpose. A focused first version ships in weeks, your team uses it for real, and we sharpen it from there. If we think an off-the-shelf tool genuinely fits your case, we'll say so on the call — a systems audit is often the right first step when you're not sure what to build. And when the software is running, it becomes the foundation for AI and automation that removes the manual work entirely.

Questions

Asked before the call.

How much does custom software cost?

It's scoped and priced fixed after a free discovery call. A focused internal tool costs far less than most owners expect — often less than a year of the SaaS subscriptions it replaces. You'll have a number within 48 hours of the call.

Why not just use off-the-shelf software?

If an off-the-shelf tool genuinely fits, we'll tell you to buy it. Custom makes sense when you're paying for five tools that don't talk to each other, bending your process around someone else's product, or running the business on spreadsheets that only one person understands.

Can it integrate with the tools we already use?

Yes — that's usually the point. Stripe, QuickBooks, Google Workspace, calendars, email, SMS, and most anything with an API. We build around your existing stack, not against it.

Who owns the software when it's done?

You do — code, data, and accounts. No lock-in, no license fees back to us. We stay available for support and iteration, but the system is yours.

How long does a build take?

Most internal tools ship a working first version in weeks. We default to getting something real into your team's hands early, then sharpening it against actual daily use.
