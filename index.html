06 / How we work

Four rules. Checkable.

01

Plain language.

If we can't explain a decision without jargon, we don't understand it well enough yet.

02

Artifacts over updates.

You see the commits, the deploys and the running thing. Not a status email saying it's going well.

03

Scope in writing.

Fixed scope and price before work starts. A change gets priced before it gets built.

04

One person, end to end.

The person on the call is the person reading your code. No account managers, no handoffs.

07 / Questions

Asked before the call.

What exactly do I get from an audit?

A written report and a walkthrough call. What has been built, what state it's in, whether the estimates on the table hold up, and what we'd do next. Plain language throughout.

I already have a developer. Is this going behind their back?

No. Most audits go better with the developer in the loop. You're getting a second opinion you can understand, not a replacement.

Can't I just ask ChatGPT?

It will comment on any code you paste in. It won't read the whole repo, the commit history and the invoices together. It won't tell you what to ask your developer on Monday.

What does a build cost?

Most land between $5k and $40k. The scope and the price are written down before any work starts.

Who does the work?

Edward Jones, the founder. The person on the call is the person reading your code.

Can you work on the system we already have?

Yes. A lot of the work starts there. We audit what exists, fix what's broken, and add AI where it saves time or brings in revenue. No rebuild unless the audit says you need one.

Do I own what you build?

Yes. The code and the accounts are yours.
